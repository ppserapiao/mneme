01 — The mark

A stack. Three layers. One hot.

The mneme mark reads as a memory stack: layered context, with the active layer surfaced in orange. Matte fills, slight grain — never glossy, never gradients.

Clearspace = 1× bar-height

Construction

  • Built on a 512 × 512 grid.
  • Three rounded bars (r = 16). Bottom 320w · middle 256w · top 176w · 72h each, gap 32.
  • Top bar is #E0651D. Lower two are #15110F.
  • Subtle grain (SVG feTurbulence) multiplied at ~55% opacity for matte texture.

Minimums

  • Digital · 24px square (16px for favicon only — drop the grain).
  • Print · 10mm square.
  • Clearspace · the height of one bar on all four sides.

05 — Spacing & geometry

Four-pixel base. Generous rhythm.

Multiples of 4 — but lean toward larger steps (24, 32, 48) for breathing room. The brand reads calm; tight density is reserved for data-dense UI.

Texture

Every primary surface carries a low-contrast SVG noise overlay (feTurbulence baseFreq 0.85, multiply ~10%). The mark itself uses a denser grain (baseFreq 1.4, ~55%). This is the brand's signature — never ship a flat surface without it.

07 — Voice

Direct. Plainspoken. Slightly editorial.

We write like an essay, not a sales page. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No "revolutionary." No "AI-powered." When we make a claim, we name a mechanism.

Sounds like us

  • "Your memory. Your keys. Every model."
  • "Memory that belongs to you."
  • "Stored on your device. Synced when you choose."
  • "The memory layer the AI era forgot to build."
  • "Open protocol. Client-held keys. Cross-provider."

Not us

  • "Revolutionary AI-powered memory platform"
  • "Unlock the power of your context"
  • "The future of personal AI is here"
  • "Seamlessly leverage your data"
  • "World-class enterprise-grade solution"
